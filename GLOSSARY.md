# The Open Movement

Vocabulary for the interactive calibre exhibit. The project uses watchmaking language precisely, because the point of the product is to teach that language.

## The object

**Calibre MW-01**:
The fictional, illustrative mechanical movement modelled by this project. It is a 4 Hz manual-wind skeleton calibre with a Swiss lever escapement.
_Avoid_: watch model, product

**Movement**:
The complete mechanism that keeps time, excluding the case and strap.
_Avoid_: engine, mechanism

**Calibre**:
A specific movement design identified by its layout, dimensions and functions. MW-01 is a calibre.
_Avoid_: model, version

**Skeleton**:
A movement whose plates and bridges are cut away so the mechanism is visible from the dial side.
_Avoid_: open-worked, transparent

## Structure

**Main plate**:
The foundation plate on which the wheels, bridges and jewels are mounted.
_Avoid_: base plate, chassis

**Bridge**:
A shaped metal strip fixed over the plate that holds one or more wheel arbors in jewels.
_Avoid_: bracket, support

**Cock**:
A small bridge with one free end, such as the balance cock.
_Avoid_: arm, mount

**Wheel**:
A toothed disc that transmits rotation. Wheels are paired with pinions to change speed.
_Avoid_: gear, cog

**Pinion**:
The small toothed cylinder that a wheel drives. Counting wheel teeth against pinion leaves gives the gear ratio.
_Avoid_: small gear, driver

**Arbor**:
The axle on which a wheel or pinion turns.
_Avoid_: shaft, spindle

**Jewel**:
A synthetic ruby bearing that reduces friction at an arbor pivot.
_Avoid_: bearing, ruby

**Chaton**:
The metal ring that holds a jewel in a plate or bridge.
_Avoid_: bezel, setting

## Power and transmission

**Mainspring**:
The coiled spring inside the barrel that stores the watch's energy.
_Avoid_: spring, power source

**Barrel**:
The drum that houses the mainspring and carries the barrel wheel.
_Avoid_: drum, canister

**Gear train**:
The sequence of wheels and pinions that carries energy from the barrel to the escapement.
_Avoid_: transmission, drivetrain

**Motion works**:
The wheels between the center wheel and the hour hand that create the 12:1 hour reduction.
_Avoid_: dial train, hands mechanism

**Keyless works**:
The mechanism that connects the crown to winding and hand-setting.
_Avoid_: winding mechanism, stem works

**Ratchet wheel**:
The toothed wheel on the barrel arbor that the crown wheel drives to wind the mainspring.
_Avoid_: winding wheel

**Click**:
The spring-loaded pawl that stops the ratchet wheel from unwinding.
_Avoid_: pawl, lock

**Power reserve**:
The remaining running time stored in the mainspring, expressed in hours.
_Avoid_: charge, battery

## Regulation

**Escapement**:
The mechanism that releases the gear train one tooth at a time and gives the balance its impulse.
_Avoid_: release mechanism, ticker

**Swiss lever escapement**:
The lever escapement used in MW-01, with a pallet fork pivoting between the escape wheel and the balance.
_Avoid_: anchor escapement

**Pallet fork**:
The lever that locks and unlocks the escape wheel and pushes the balance.
_Avoid_: anchor, lever

**Pallet stone**:
A ruby block set into the pallet fork that contacts the escape wheel teeth.
_Avoid_: jewel, pallet

**Escape wheel**:
The last wheel of the train, released one half-tooth per beat by the pallet fork.
_Avoid_: escape gear

**Balance wheel**:
The weighted oscillator that swings back and forth and divides time into equal beats.
_Avoid_: flywheel, oscillator

**Hairspring**:
The flat spiral spring that returns the balance wheel and sets its frequency.
_Avoid_: balance spring, coil

**Beat**:
One half-oscillation of the balance; the escape wheel advances once per beat.
_Avoid_: tick, cycle

**Beat rate**:
Beats per hour. MW-01 runs at 28,800 vph, or 4 Hz.
_Avoid_: frequency, tick rate

**Amplitude**:
The angular swing of the balance from centre to one extreme, in degrees.
_Avoid_: swing, angle

## Finishing and display

**Anglage**:
The hand-chamfered, polished bevel along the edge of a bridge or plate.
_Avoid_: chamfer, bevel

**Côtes de Genève**:
Parallel, slightly curved bands of graining applied to bridges.
_Avoid_: stripes, brushing

**Perlage**:
Overlapping circular graining spots applied to the main plate.
_Avoid_: dots, stippling

**Chapter ring**:
The ring carrying the hour markers, separated from the movement so the mechanism stays visible.
_Avoid_: dial, face

**Exploded view**:
A presentation in which assembly layers separate along the watch axis to show how the movement is built.
_Avoid_: breakdown, assembly view

## The experience

**Inspection**:
The act of pointing at a component to reveal its name, purpose and specification.
_Avoid_: hover, tooltip

**Inspector**:
The fixed information panel that describes the inspected component.
_Avoid_: card, popup, tooltip

**Pin**:
To lock the current inspection so the camera can move without losing the selection.
_Avoid_: select, lock

**Tour**:
The six-stop guided sequence that explains the power path and the display.
_Avoid_: walkthrough, story

**Train clock**:
The simulation clock derived from the display clock and the gear ratios, used to rotate the wheels.
_Avoid_: gear animation

**Escapement clock**:
The 4 Hz simulation clock that drives the balance, pallet fork and escape wheel.
_Avoid_: beat clock
